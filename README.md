# intent-gateway

A self-hosted Docker gateway that gives AI agents real shopping under a privacy policy the developer controls. The agent searches and builds carts within a YAML policy, the buyer approves each purchase with a passkey on their phone and pays on the merchant's own checkout, and the developer can verify exactly what data left their machine.

**Status: in development, not usable yet.** The repository stays private until its launch gate is met: a first real order, a published threat model and `SECURITY.md`, and live spend caps and rate limits.
